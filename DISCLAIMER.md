# Disclaimer (DRM Removal)

Onleiharr can optionally call a third-party tool (libgourou) to remove DRM
from downloaded PDF files. This capability is disabled by default and must be
explicitly enabled by the user.

This is not legal advice. You are solely responsible for complying with the laws
in your jurisdiction regarding DRM removal. The legality of DRM removal for
private use varies by country and circumstances.

Onleiharr does not include DRM removal code. It only invokes external tools
provided by libgourou, which is not part of the Onleiharr project.

The Onleiharr developers and contributors accept no liability for any misuse,
damages, or legal consequences arising from the use of DRM removal features.
